## Командный мостик

Файл: `concept-command-bridge.png`.

> Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: polished future desktop design concept for an existing Russian-language browser tabletop game, Star Wars starship battles. Create a single widescreen 16:9 high-fidelity, plausible-to-build game interface screenshot, not poster art. Layout: slim tactical-command header; left narrow fleet roster with six legible ship rows and status bars; expansive central dark 3D hex battlefield with six realistic recognizable sci-fi starship miniatures; right information panel showing one selected ship, its larger hex token, and exact six arc values. Current game has three columns and we are refining it, not replacing layout. Key innovation: on the board each ship hex has exactly six thin short luminous bars, one centered on each hex SIDE and entirely INSIDE the hex; no plus/minus glyphs or numeric strength values on the battlefield. Bars encode negative coral red, zero slate gray, +1 pale blue, +2 cyan, +3 or more mint green. One tiny readable legend above the field. An amber notch indicates ship facing independently of strength color. In right panel exact signed values +3, +2, +1, 0, -1 appear clearly INSIDE the six sides of a magnified hex, not at vertices, around the selected rendered ship. Strong Star Wars space-naval feeling through restrained brushed gunmetal, cold holographic blue and warm amber instrument accents, subtle volumetric light and tactile frames; not gaudy neon. Top-down-oblique tactical view, precise spacing, strong contrast and readable hierarchy. Render existing-looking 3D model ships as distinct silhouettes, not icon substitutes. Russian UI labels limited to 'ЭПОХИ И ФРАКЦИИ', 'ФЛОТ', 'ХОД', 'ВЫБРАНО', 'СИЛА ГРАНИ'. No other legible prose; no random numbers/text/watermark. Avoid pop-up cards over board, overlapping marks, numbers spilling outside hexes, cinematic poster composition, excessive glow.

## Тактический HUD

Файл: `concept-tactical-hud.png`.

> Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: second polished future desktop design concept for the existing Russian Star Wars starship hex battle browser game. Make this variant deliberately more practical and easier to implement than a cinematic space poster: realistic widescreen game screenshot, crisp 1440p desktop UI, detailed but quiet 3D materials and deep navy/graphite tactical hologram. Preserve actual three-column game layout: compact left fleet status list, large center 61-cell hex board with six small 3D ships, right stacked command buttons and selected-ship inspector. Central playfield should occupy about 60% of width, with a clean, subdued space backdrop behind visible hexes; do not let scenery interfere with play. Each occupied hex has exactly six tiny strength indicators, one on the MIDPOINT OF EACH SIDE, INSIDE the hex outline: negative coral, zero muted slate, +1 pale blue, +2 cyan, +3+ mint. No numbers or signs over the board, no hovering strength plaques, and no indicators at hex vertices. Use a compact legend on the edge of the board. Selected ship is highlighted with a restrained white/gold outline, not a large glow. In right inspector a precise enlarged schematic of the same hex shows six signed numbers clearly INSIDE side midpoints and a central top-view 3D ship; under it, readable hull, shield, charge and attack stats. Upper right 'ХОД' and command controls remain easy to find. Warm amber marks faction A and cool electric blue marks faction B, while sector-strength colors never change with faction. Top bar title exact 'ЭПОХИ И ФРАКЦИИ'; panel headings exact 'ФЛОТ', 'КОМАНДЫ', 'ВЫБРАНО'; all other text may be abstract UI texture, not gibberish. Visual language: Star Wars naval bridge display, brushed durasteel, inset panels, dimensional shadows, precise technical typography, no logos, no watermarks, no characters, no giant planet taking over the board. Strong visual hierarchy, readable at normal monitor distance, realistic to build in HTML/CSS/WebGL.
